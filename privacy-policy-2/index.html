Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 6/8/2025

AllHighForever (“we”, “our”, or “us”) operates the website allhighforever.org, where we provide cannabis-related travel guides and offer CBD/THC product recommendations via affiliate links and dropshipping partners. This privacy policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Who we are

Our website address is: https://allhighforever.org
We are located in the United States. You may contact us at: [email protected]

What personal data we collect and why

Comments

If you leave a comment, we collect the data shown in the comment form, your IP address, and your browser user agent string to help spam detection. If you use Gravatar, an anonymized string (hash) may be sent to that service to display your profile picture.

Purchases (WooCommerce)

If you make a purchase, we collect your name, billing/shipping address, email, and payment information. This data is used to process your order and provide customer service.

Newsletter Signup (MailPoet or similar)

If you subscribe to our email list, your name and email are collected for the purpose of sending marketing content and updates. You may opt out at any time using the unsubscribe link.

Contact Forms

If you contact us via forms, we collect the data you provide, including your name, email address, and message content. This data is stored temporarily for customer service purposes.

Cookies

Cookies help enhance your experience by remembering preferences, login sessions, and analytics.

Cookies are used to:

  • Maintain login sessions
  • Track affiliate link usage
  • Monitor site traffic via analytics
  • Show ads via third-party services (e.g. Google, Meta)

You may disable cookies in your browser at any time.

Embedded content from other websites

Articles may include embedded content (e.g. YouTube videos or product widgets). These behave the same as visiting the third-party site and may collect tracking data.

Analytics & Advertising

We use third-party tools such as Google Analytics and Mantis Ad Network. These tools may collect anonymized data such as your IP address, location, or browser behavior.

For personalized ads and affiliate link tracking, cookies may be used. You may opt out of some services via browser settings or https://optout.networkadvertising.org/.

Who we share your data with

  • Email provider (for newsletters)
  • Payment processors (if purchases are made)
  • Affiliate partners (via outbound tracking links)
  • Ad networks (e.g., Google Ads, Mantis)

We do not sell your data.

How long we retain your data

If you leave a comment, it and its metadata are retained indefinitely.

Registered users can view, edit, or delete their personal info at any time (except the username). Admins can also edit that data.

Your rights

You can request an exported file of your personal data or request we erase any data we hold. This excludes any data we are obligated to retain for legal, administrative, or security reasons.

Where your data is sent

Data may be processed in the United States or wherever our service providers are located. Comments may be screened by spam detection services.

How we protect your data

We use SSL encryption, secure servers, and restrict data access to authorized personnel only.

Questions?

If you have questions about our privacy practices, contact us at: [email protected]
