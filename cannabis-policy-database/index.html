Minnesota's legislature passed the SF 4401 omnibus cannabis bill on May 17, 2026, sending it to Governor Tim Walz, who is expected to sign. The bill doubles individual THC serving limits to 10 mg, quadruples retail package caps to 200 mg, and from August 1 permits large-format hemp beverages (750 ml+, up to 5 mg THC per serving). It also merges the medical and adult-use supply chains, simplifying what visiting patients and recreational shoppers see at retail.

Montana House Bill 636 reduces the maximum THC per serving in ingestible cannabis products from 10mg to 5mg, effective July 1, 2026; products currently labeled above 5mg per serving cannot be sold after that date. Travelers purchasing edibles in Montana before July 1 may still find 10mg servings, but products on shelves after that date must comply with the new lower limit.

Louisiana makes cannabis use near schools a felony

The Louisiana legislature passed HB 568 in a 23-10 Senate vote, making it a felony to smoke, vape, or consume cannabis on…

The Louisiana legislature passed HB 568 in a 23-10 Senate vote, making it a felony to smoke, vape, or consume cannabis on or within 2,000 feet of any school or university campus, punishable by up to one year in jail and a $1,000 fine, with no probation or parole. Governor Jeff Landry, whose staff lobbied for the bill, is expected to sign it. Travelers passing through Louisiana college towns or near schools should be aware that consumption in these zones now carries severe criminal penalties on top of the state's existing recreational prohibition.

Georgia Governor Brian Kemp signed SB 220 on May 12, 2026, significantly expanding the state's medical cannabis program. The law removes the previous 5% THC potency cap (replacing it with a 12,000mg patient possession limit), adds inflammatory bowel disease and lupus as qualifying conditions, and permits vaporization for patients aged 21 and over. Medical cannabis cardholders visiting Georgia will find broader product access, though the state remains medical-only with no recreational market.
